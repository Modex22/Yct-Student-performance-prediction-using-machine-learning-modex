Identify students who may be at risk now

The purpose of this lecturer workflow is to highlight students who may need support based on marks already recorded—not to predict their final scores.

What the analyzer currently supports

Photo OCR creates an editable draft, but the existing analyzer still estimates final scores from four inputs: exam, test/CA, assignment and practical. It does not yet screen course-by-course marks or cumulative GPA directly for current risk. Check OCR carefully, and don’t treat its current predictions as a current-risk assessment for sheets like the examples.

Images are processed locally in this browser and are not uploaded. OCR may misread names and marks, so verify the extracted draft before using it.
