Objective

Purpose

This system predicts a student's likely final score and identifies students who may be at risk. It supports academic planning and early intervention through continuous-assessment and factor-based predictions.

  • Estimate academic performance from assessment scores
  • Identify pass/fail and at-risk outcomes
  • Support lecturers with class-level roster analysis
  • Provide optional personal performance history
Implementation

Technology Stack

  • Programming language: Python 3
  • Machine learning: Scikit-learn
  • Web framework: Flask
  • Data processing: Pandas and NumPy
  • Visualization: Matplotlib and server-rendered charts
  • Persistence: SQLite
Process

Prediction Methodology

01

Prepare data

Assessment values are validated and standardized for model input.

02

Train models

Regression and classification models are trained and cross-validated.

03

Generate prediction

The system estimates a score, grade band, and pass/fail outcome.

04

Support decisions

Results provide recommendations and at-risk guidance for academic follow-up.

Classification bands

Predicted scores are mapped to Fail / Pass / Lower Credit / Upper Credit / Distinction — the Nigerian-polytechnic convention — using illustrative cutoffs (45 / 50 / 60 / 70) modelled loosely on common NBTE banding. These have not been confirmed against Yabatech's official grading policy; edit CLASSIFICATION_BINS in src/config.py once you know the real cutoffs.
